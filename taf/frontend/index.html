How it Works

This system uses Archimedes' Principle to calculate the density of a sample. When an object is weighed in air and then in water, the difference in weight is equal to the buoyant force, which is the weight of the water displaced by the object.

Formula: Density = (Weight in Air × Water Density) / (Weight in Air - Weight in Water)

Note: Water density is assumed to be 1.0 g/cm³ at room temperature.
